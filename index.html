How to play

You start 5 metres ahead of the ridgeback. Gain 1 metre of lead for every 16 metres of clean running. When you fall, the ridgeback gains 10 metres while you get back up. If it reaches you first, the run ends. Earn 1 point per metre, 1 per obstacle passed, and 10 per gem. Follow the high gems as you jump and the low gems as you duck.

At a turn sign, swipe in the direction of the arrow before you reach the 90° corner. Elsewhere, swipe sideways to switch lanes. Follow raised gems across breaks in the trail. At a zipline, line up with the grip and jump to cross the ravine. Run through each arch to enter the next level. Your score and lead carry over. After the sixth level, the route circles back to Sāmoa.
