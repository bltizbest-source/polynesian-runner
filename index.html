How to play

You start 30 metres ahead of the ridgeback. Gain 1 metre of lead for every 10 metres of clean running. When you fall, the ridgeback gains 10 metres while you get back up. If it reaches you first, the run ends. Earn 1 point per metre, 1 per obstacle passed, and 10 per gem. Follow the high gems as you jump and the low gems as you duck.
